# News Indicators

News should be evaluated across five dimensions:

1. Relevance: whether the news truly relates to the asset or AI tech theme.
2. Sentiment direction: bullish, neutral, or bearish.
3. Event strength: ordinary update or major catalyst.
4. Coverage breadth: single-name or sector-wide.
5. Freshness: new information or repeated coverage.

Primary catalysts:

- Earnings and guidance.
- AI chip and compute demand.
